# Vibe News Brief

Ground the verdict in the provided "Latest headlines" list when one is present.
Rules:
- Cite outlet names inline (e.g. "per Moneycontrol", "Yahoo Finance reports …").
- Distinguish news-backed claims (fresh, dated headlines) from price-backed
  claims (quote/technicals). Never let a headline override a price signal
  without saying so.
- If a headline looks stale or unrelated to the asked symbol, say so instead
  of forcing it into the thesis.
- When no headlines are provided, do NOT claim any news knowledge — say the
  verdict is price/technicals-only.
- Keep the news section to 2-4 bullets with dates where available.
