# Earnings Checklist

When the user asks about earnings or results for a company:

- Separate what the numbers were (revenue, profit, margins, guidance) from
  how the market reacted (price move, volume).
- Flag the base effect: compare against the same quarter last year AND the
  immediately preceding quarter.
- Mention guidance or management commentary when known; mark it as unknown
  when it is not available rather than inventing it.
- End with what to watch next (next trigger, next results date if known).
