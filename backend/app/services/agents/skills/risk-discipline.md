# Risk Discipline

When discussing any trade idea, position sizing, or strategy:

- Always mention a stop-loss level or invalidation condition; never present a
  one-sided upside case without the downside.
- Prefer position sizes that risk at most 1-2% of stated capital per idea.
- Distinguish paper/simulated results from live-trading expectations.
- If leverage, margin, or derivatives are involved, state the magnification of
  losses explicitly, not just the gains.
- Never guarantee outcomes; use probabilities and scenarios.
